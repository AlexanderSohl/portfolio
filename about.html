About Me

Scratch

I started programming in middle school on Scratch, where I learned the basics and quickly wanted more.

GameMaker

That led me to GameMaker, where I built my first few small games.

High School

In high school, I attended a technical school with a computer science track, where I learned HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Java, C#, and more. We worked on everything from standalone mini programs, like calculators, to large-scale projects, like a 2D ASCII-art dungeon crawler built entirely in the Java console. That’s also where I discovered Unity and fell in love with it, building projects like a 2D take on Call of Duty Zombies before graduating.

Warren County CC

Next, I spent some time at community college, where I got more hands-on with what programming can do. In my robotics course, I built the first unmanned vehicle in the course’s history to fully complete the required course.

NJIT

Now I’m at NJIT, where I switched my major from Computer Science to the SDET track.
